What are the quality criteria for Jawhara Jewellery Diamonds (4Cs)?

Diamonds come in various colours, from "colourless" diamonds, classified on an internationally recognized scale, to rare hues like yellow, blue, pink, and green. The quality of a diamond is determined by the perfect balance of the "4 Cs," which make each stone unique: Clarity: This measures the number, size, position, and colour of inclusions visible within the diamond. Clarity is evaluated with a 10x magnifying glass. Jawhara Diamonds range from Flawless (Fl) to Very Slightly Included (VS-SI). Colour: The value of a diamond increases with its lack of colour. The more colourless a diamond, the rarer and more precious it is. Jawhara Diamonds are available in colours ranging from D (totally colourless) to I. This grading scale uses these letters to indicate the presence of any yellow or brown tints. Cut: The cut of a diamond affects its brilliance, fire, and scintillation. It is the only quality criterion that depends entirely on human skill. Excellent cuts have precise proportions and symmetrical facets that enhance the diamond's sparkle. Jawhara Diamonds are available in nearly all shapes, including Round Brilliant, Princess, Pear, Emerald, Heart, Oval, Marquise, Cushion, Baguette, and Asscher. Carat: The carat measures a diamond's weight, with one carat equivalent to 0.20 grams. The term "carat" originates from the carob tree seeds historically used for weighing gemstones. Jawhara Jewellery offers diamonds ranging from 1 to 200 carats.
